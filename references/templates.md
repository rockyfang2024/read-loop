# Output templates

## Whole-book decomposition (default)

```markdown
# <Book title> — complete decomposition

**Author:** <name>
**Book type:** <type; explicit / inferred / uncertain>
**Source coverage:** <chapters or sections reviewed; extraction gaps>
**Spoiler scope:** Full-book analysis, including ending

## Through-line
<Central question, premise, or problem and the book's overall answer or movement>

## Structure
| Part / chapter | Role in the whole | Key developments or claims |
|---|---|---|

## Main analysis
<Adapt to genre: for fiction, plot arc, character relationships/development, narrative choices, themes, ending; for nonfiction, concepts, claims, reasoning, evidence, assumptions, applications>

## Key people / concepts
<Introduce and connect the people or concepts essential to understanding the book>

## Themes, tensions, and turning points
<Recurring ideas, disagreements, changes, and moments that reshape the whole>

## Critical assessment
<What the book supports well, where its evidence or interpretation is limited, and what remains unresolved>

## Questions for the reader
<Questions that deepen understanding; provide answers too when requested>

## Source trail
<Chapter and section references for the main findings>
```

## Book map

```markdown
# <Book title> — reading map

**Author:** <name>  
**Book type:** <type; explicit / inferred / uncertain><br>
**Reader's goal:** <goal or assumed goal>  
**Coverage:** <full source / selected chapters / excerpts only>

## Central question
<What problem does the book address?>

## Thesis
<Author's thesis, with source location and uncertainty if needed>

## Structure
| Part/chapter | Role in the argument | Priority for this reader |
|---|---|---|

## Recurring ideas and disagreements
<Concepts, frameworks, positions that conflict, and the conditions that distinguish them>

## Timeline suitability
<Whether chronology is central or useful; explain briefly. For chronology-rich books, include the timeline below.>

## Key-event timeline
<Include only when chronology is central or useful. Cite chapter/section for each entry.>

| Date / period | Event | Why it matters | Source location | Date confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <exact, approximate, or range> | <concise event> | <role in the book's arc or argument> | <chapter / section> | <explicit / approximate / disputed> |

## Suggested route
<Short, medium, or deep route; what it skips and the resulting limits>

## Extraction gaps / open questions
<Images, tables, missing chapters, uncertain headings, or claims needing verification>
```

## Pre-reading prompt

```markdown
### Before reading: <chapter/section>
**Why this section matters:** <one or two sentences>

1. <Question requiring explanation or reasoning>
2. <Question requiring evidence, comparison, or application>

Don't look for a perfect answer yet. What do you currently expect, and why?
```

Do not append suggested answers to this prompt unless the reader requests an answer-first mode.

## Chapter card

```markdown
### <Chapter title> — <author if relevant>
- **Core idea:** <one sentence in the reader's own words>
- **Author's claim:** <claim, with chapter/section reference>
- **Evidence in the book:** <data, example, reasoning, or anecdote the author uses>
- **Conditions / limits:** <where it applies and where it may not>
- **My application:** <decision, action, or case>
- **Open question:** <what remains unclear or could change the conclusion>
- **Review prompts:** <one to three retrieval questions, answers omitted>
```

## Claim comparison

```markdown
| Author / position | Claim | Evidence offered | Assumptions and conditions | Trade-off / open question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```

## End-of-session note

```markdown
## Session — <date>
- **I can now explain:** <one or two ideas>
- **I still confuse / need to check:** <specific gap>
- **Next reading:** <chapter or section>
- **Next recall:** <question, without answer>
- **Review:** <suggested next date or interval>
```
